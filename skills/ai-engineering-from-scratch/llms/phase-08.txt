## Flagged: read the note first

- [01 Generative Models — Taxonomy & History](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch/1a8c4fd/phases/08-generative-ai/01-generative-models-taxonomy-history/docs/en.md): WRONG: Doc's printed p(x in [-0.5,0.5]) of ~0.38 (histogram) and ~0.41 (KDE) is wrong; the mixture puts only ~0.03 mass there.
- [02 Autoencoders & Variational Autoencoders (VAE)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch/1a8c4fd/phases/08-generative-ai/02-autoencoders-vae/docs/en.md): WRONG: Doc says the SD VAE has a 32x spatial-area downsample; 512 to 64 is 8x per axis (64x area), as the lesson's own 1024 to 128 numbers show.
- [06 Diffusion Models — DDPM from Scratch](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch/1a8c4fd/phases/08-generative-ai/06-diffusion-ddpm-from-scratch/docs/en.md): WRONG: Doc says SDXL uses v-prediction; SDXL base's scheduler config sets prediction_type to epsilon.
- [07 Latent Diffusion & Stable Diffusion](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch/1a8c4fd/phases/08-generative-ai/07-latent-diffusion-stable-diffusion/docs/en.md): WRONG: Doc says latents are 1/16th the pixels, but 512x512 to 64x64 is 64x fewer positions; Flux.1-Kontext is dated Dec 2024 but released May 29, 2025.
- [08 ControlNet, LoRA & Conditioning](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch/1a8c4fd/phases/08-generative-ai/08-controlnet-lora-conditioning/docs/en.md): WRONG: Doc gives ControlNet sizes as ~360M for SDXL and ~70M for SD 1.5; the SD 1.5 canny ControlNet is 361M params and the SDXL one about 1.25B.
- [09 Inpainting, Outpainting & Image Editing](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch/1a8c4fd/phases/08-generative-ai/09-inpainting-outpainting-editing/docs/en.md): stale: Lists DALL-E 3 as an inpainting model; it is removed from the API (and may never have supported edits)
- [10 Video Generation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch/1a8c4fd/phases/08-generative-ai/10-video-generation/docs/en.md): stale: Recommends Sora as a hosted top text-to-video option; OpenAI shut down the Sora 2 models and Videos API on 2026-09-24, before today.
- [11 Audio Generation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch/1a8c4fd/phases/08-generative-ai/11-audio-generation/docs/en.md): WRONG: Says 24 kHz audio at 2 kbps uses 8 RVQ codebooks at 75 Hz; that is 8 x 10 bits x 75 Hz = 6 kbps (Encodec's 6 kbps setting).
- [13 Flow Matching & Rectified Flows](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch/1a8c4fd/phases/08-generative-ai/13-flow-matching-rectified-flows/docs/en.md): WRONG: Sign error: d/dt of a(t)x0 + s(t)x1 is a'x0 + s'x1, so v = a'x0 - s'x1 is wrong and contradicts the doc's own dx/dt = x1 - x0.
- [14 Evaluation — FID, CLIP Score, Human Preference](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch/1a8c4fd/phases/08-generative-ai/14-evaluation-fid-clip-score/docs/en.md): WRONG: HPSv2 was trained on 798k preference choices; 107k is the prompt count. PickScore's Pick-a-Pic v1 is about 0.5M examples, not 2.6M.
- [19 Visual Autoregressive Modeling (VAR): Next-Scale Prediction](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rohitg00/ai-engineering-from-scratch/1a8c4fd/phases/08-generative-ai/19-visual-autoregressive-var/docs/en.md): WRONG: HART is discrete AR tokens plus a continuous residual learned by a small diffusion module; it is not MaskGIT-style decoding of a VAR variant.
